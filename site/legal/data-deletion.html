Delete Your Data

Applies to both LoyaltyCards (Customer Wallet) and LoyaltyCards Business, published by Ian Hamlet  ·  Last Updated: September 6, 2026

There is nothing to request. Neither app has an account, a server, or any storage outside your own device - deleting the app deletes everything, immediately and completely.

How to delete your data

  1. Find the app on your device (LoyaltyCards or LoyaltyCards Business)
  2. Uninstall it, the same way you'd remove any other app (long-press the icon and choose Uninstall/Delete, or via your device's Settings → Apps)

That's it. There's no account to close, no support ticket to raise, and nothing on our side to action - uninstalling is the deletion, because your data was never stored anywhere but that device.

What gets deleted

LoyaltyCards (Customer Wallet): every loyalty card, stamp count, and transaction timestamp held on your device.

LoyaltyCards Business: your business configuration, cryptographic signing keys, and local transaction/audit history held on your device.

What's retained, and for how long

Nothing, for any length of time. We have no servers and no backend - there has never been a copy of your data anywhere except your own device, so there is nothing left over to delete on our end after you uninstall.

The one exception is entirely under your own control: if you (as a business owner) chose to save a Recovery Backup or Clone QR code - printed, emailed, or saved to a file - that copy exists because you created and stored it yourself. Uninstalling the app has no effect on copies you made; deleting those is up to you, wherever you put them.
